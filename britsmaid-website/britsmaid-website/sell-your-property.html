The Sell + Stay Approach

A property that sells itself, literally.

A vacant listing is a hard sell. A property with a live booking calendar and a proven income record is a different conversation entirely.

While it's on the market

Your property stays under Britsmaid management — bookings, upkeep and guest handling continue exactly as they do now. You keep earning through the entire sale process.

For the buyer

Prospective buyers see a property with a real occupancy and revenue history — and can experience it as a guest before they commit. That's a stronger pitch than an empty villa.

How It Works

From listing to sale, without pausing income

  1. Valuation conversation

    We review the property's booking history, occupancy and current condition together.

  2. Listed for sale

    The property is marketed for sale while remaining live for bookings under Britsmaid management.

  3. Buyer enquiries

    Interested buyers can view the property, and optionally experience it as a guest stay first.

  4. Sale completes

    Management continues under new ownership if the buyer chooses to keep it as a rental, or transitions out cleanly if not.
